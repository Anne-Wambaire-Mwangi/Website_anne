Who I Am

Anne Wambaire Mwangi

I am a certified Business Intelligence Analyst with a background in Economics, passionate about transforming data into meaningful insights and impact. My expertise lies at the intersection of Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, and Data Science, where I enjoy solving problems, uncovering patterns, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

Driven by both curiosity and purpose, I believe data is most powerful when used to improve lives, organizations, and communities. Beyond my technical work, I am deeply passionate about service and leadership. During my time at Nova Scotia Community College, I served as an International Student Ambassador and Cultural Coordinator, helping foster community, connection, and support for students from diverse backgrounds.

Outside of work and academics, I enjoy playing chess, following and analyzing financial markets, and exploring the world of trading. These interests continue to strengthen my strategic thinking, analytical mindset, and curiosity about global trends.
